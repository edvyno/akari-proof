x402 field guide

Five lessons from grading 791 sellers and running our own paid endpoints. Every number here was measured by calling the route once, unauthenticated, with no payment.

01 — the 200-on-a-paid-path defect

Of 645 sellers that advertise a paid route, 306 answer 200 to an unauthenticated GET. A buyer following their own documentation is never asked to pay.

curl -s -o /dev/null -w '%{http_code}' https://your-host/your-paid-path   # must be 402

Require 402 on every path you list as paid. A 200 means your paywall is not in the request path at all.

02 — where the price actually lives

The number in maxAmountRequired is the advertised ceiling. The amount a client must authorise is accepts[0].amount, in micro-USDC. Our own live challenge right now: 20000 (= $0.02) on eip155:8453.

curl -si https://your-host/your-paid-path | sed -n '/^{/,$p'   # read accepts[0].amount

Print accepts[0].amount, not the ceiling, and compare it with the price on your homepage.

03 — payable is not discoverable

Indexes match a merchant name and a URL path. Descriptions, tags and service names are stored and then ignored by the matcher. A product called “wallet risk verdict” under /analytics is invisible to the agent searching for exactly that.

GET /discovery/search?query=<intent>&limit=20   # 20 is the max; 30 returns HTTP 400

Ship an intent-shaped alias path that stays payable, and keep the description for humans.

04 — agents buy tools, not homepages

242 of 791 sellers we graded serve a live MCP endpoint. The paid call is a tools/call with the payment attached under params._meta['x402/payment'].

POST /mcp {"method":"tools/call","params":{"name":"price","_meta":{"x402/payment":…}}}

If initialize and tools/list answer but a paid tools/call does not, you have a demo, not a product.

05 — publish your own numbers

Our lifetime external revenue is $0.06 from 3 buyers. 51 of 54 settled payments were our own test settles.

SELECT count(DISTINCT payer) FROM sales WHERE status='settled' AND payer NOT IN (your_wallets)

Count distinct payers, exclude your own wallets, print the last sale date. If it embarrasses you, that is the number to work on.
