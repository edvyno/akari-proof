What it is for

  • A directory or facilitator that wants to know which sellers to index first, and which are quietly broken.
  • An infrastructure team that needs a benchmark set of live x402 sellers to test a client against.
  • Anyone deciding what tooling to build: 232 hosts lack the discovery file, 110 lack a catalog — that is the shape of the gap.
  • A research or BD list with a per-host grade and a date instead of a scraped logo wall.

What it is not

It is not a ranking of product quality or revenue, and it is not for sale to sellers to improve their grade. The grade is the probe. Grades measure payment plumbing and discoverability: whether a machine buyer can find you, decode your price, and settle. Nothing about a host's grade can be bought, and no row is deleted on request — a host that asks not to be listed gets a note saying so, not a fake A.
